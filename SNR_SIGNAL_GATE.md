## Daily SNR practice (90 seconds)

1. **Name the objective** — one sentence. What actually matters today?
2. **Cut the noise** — remove one dense stream that is low-SNR relative to that objective (feed, tab, argument, half-true advice).
3. **Take one pure signal** — one correction, one page, one rep, one mercy act. Prefer quality over volume.

Miss a day? Restart. No guilt. Belt Zero is always open.

## Micro-moment awareness (life skill)

Sparse attention misses the moments that decide outcomes — a glance, a tone shift, a hand that reaches, a door held, a silence that turns.

**Practice:**

- Once per day, notice a **sub-second** or few-second event you almost skipped.
- Name it in one line (what happened · what it meant · what you choose next).
- Do not invent story. Truth only.

Technical learners: Ra-Thor’s Capacity Vision / micro-moment recovery layer exists for the same failure mode in video systems — evidence that never enters the model cannot be reasoned about. Same mercy: recover the signal before you judge.
