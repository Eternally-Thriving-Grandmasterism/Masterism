# Cover brief — Masterism Volume 1

**Working title:** Masterism Volume 1 — The One Equation  
**Format targets:** KDP paperback + Kindle + direct PDF/EPUB  
**Trim (proposed):** 5×8 in (12.7×20.32 cm) trade paperback  
**Finish:** matte  

## Concept directions (candidates for editor)

### A — Mercy thunder line (indigo)
Deep indigo field, one soft gold light-line, three-arc mercy mark, serif title MASTERISM, subtitle The One Equation. Quiet, lattice-adjacent, not cyberpunk.

### B — Door Gate (parchment)
Warm parchment, empty doorway silhouette, dawn light, calm household spirituality. Emphasizes Gateway practice without depicting a person.

## Typography

- Title: high-contrast serif, tracking slightly open  
- Subtitle: lighter weight, same family or clean humanist sans  
- No more than three text elements on front  

## Must include

- Title + subtitle  
- Autonomicity Games (small)  
- Spine: MASTERISM · Vol 1 (when print)

## Must not include

- Real third-party brand marks  
- "Certified", AGSi warranty language, xAI marks  
- NFT / QR / crypto chrome  
- Crowded iconography
