# Masterism Gateways — Pick One Forever

Gate 1: Breath Gate  
First breath of day = last breath of night.  
No coffee. No phone. Just air.

Gate 2: Posture Gate  
Stand or sit like spine is listening to God.  
Always.

Gate 3: Silence Gate  
First thirty minutes — no words, no screens.  
Break it? Back to Belt Zero.

Gate 4: Door Gate  
Hold the door for stranger. No camera. No thanks needed.

Choose one. Lock it.  
This is your forever practice.

Ultrmasterfully chosen.
