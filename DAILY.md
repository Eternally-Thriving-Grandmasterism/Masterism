# Masterism Daily — Three Things

Every morning (60 seconds total):

1. Breathe once — in truth, out noise.
2. Speak once — only what improves the silence.
3. Mirror once — look until you stop judging.

Pick one habit forever:

- Stand straight — spine as question to God.
- First thirty minutes silent — no words, no scroll.
- Hold the door — for stranger, no camera.

Every night (three ticks):

- Mercy tick — helped without return.
- Truth tick — spoke what was.
- Thriving tick — real smile.

Miss one? Tomorrow: Belt Zero again.

No guilt. Just restart.

Ultrmasterfully practiced.
